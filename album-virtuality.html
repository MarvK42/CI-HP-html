Album · 1992 · Lektronic Soundscapes LS-92003

Virtuality

Electronic, cyber-like structures meet virtual ethno and nature sounds to form an independent world.

The concept

Between reality and illusion.

Virtuality explores the blurred boundary between everyday reality and dream states, altered consciousness and cyberspace. Inspired by the idea of virtual worlds, the music creates images of tropical fever, machine consciousness and the mysterious Black Moon. The compositions grew through experimentation and improvisation — a creation of emotions and images.
