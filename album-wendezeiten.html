The concept

Past, presence, future?

This is our contribution to the selection for Schallplatte 25 (SP25) of schallwende e. V. on the theme “Zeitenwende”.

The piece combines past, present and future: For a long time we humans seemed to have everything under control and to rock life, even climate change seemed controllable – until the wars plunged the world into chaos and destruction. Hope lies with the children....
